## 3. Project Risk Register (`RSK-001` – `RSK-010`)

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RSK-001** | **48-Hour Free Mini Audit Bottleneck:** High influx of audit leads from face-to-face pitches or ads could overwhelm the 48-hour SLA commitment (6 checkpoints + 1 custom free sample). | Medium | High | Implement GHL Stage 1/3 automated SLA timers (alert at Hour 12 and Hour 36) and pre-build standardized Figma/Canva audit scorecard templates + sample post templates. |
| **RSK-002** | **Third-Party Script Drag on Mobile Lighthouse Performance (< 90):** Loading GHL Calendar, GTM, Meta Pixel, and Snapchat Pixel eagerly can lower Mobile Lighthouse below 90. | High | High | Use React Server Components by default, handle forms via native Next.js Server Actions (zero GHL iframe on `/free-audit`), lazy-load the GHL Calendar on `/contact`, and defer ad pixels until consent/interaction. |
| **RSK-003** | **Delay in Written Client Permission from Dawair (`OD-008`):** Dawair is pre-launch and may not have final photos or sign-off ready by Phase 1/2. | Medium | Medium | Enforce `Projects.clientPermissionVerified` gate in Payload CMS and use high-craft badged Sample Concepts (`نموذج توضيحي`) on Home and `/our-work` at launch. |
| **RSK-004** | **Unnatural or Stiff Arabic Copy:** Draft Arabic copy published without native Saudi review could feel translated rather than local to Al Ahsa shop owners. | Medium | High | Enforce mandatory pre-launch review gate (`PRE-08`) using the `Arabic Translator` CMS role and Payload Live Preview before Phase 1 and Phase 2 go live. |
| **RSK-005** | **SFDA Non-Compliance on Pharmacy Samples/Content:** Publishing unverified medical/health claims in pharmacy sample concepts could violate SFDA rules. | Low | High | Enforce `sfdaComplianceVerified` boolean gate on Pharmacy items in Payload CMS (`OPS-COMP-01`). |
| **RSK-006** | **Ephemeral Filesystem Media Loss on Container/Serverless Hosts:** Uploading media in Payload CMS to local container disk (`./media`) results in lost images when containers redeploy. | High (if unmitigated) | Critical | Mandatory use of `@payloadcms/storage-s3` connected to persistent S3-compatible object storage (**Cloudflare R2** or **AWS S3**) in Staging and Production. |
| **RSK-007** | **Trade Name Conflict (`OD-002`):** Ministry of Commerce search for `بيّورا` could require a legal suffix (e.g., `وكالة بيّورا للدعاية والإعلان`). | Medium | Low | Keep brand display name (`بيّورا / Bayyora`) distinct from the formal registered legal entity string in `SiteSettings`. |
| **RSK-008** | **Lost Leads from Invalid WhatsApp Numbers:** Shop owners typing Arabic-Indic numerals (`٠٥٥...`) or missing digits in the audit form. | Medium | Medium | Zod preprocessor in `auditFormSchema` automatically converts Arabic-Indic numerals (`٠-٩`) to standard digits (`0-9`) and validates Saudi mobile format before Server Action execution. |
| **RSK-009** | **Accidental Deletion of In-Use Media Assets by Editors:** An editor deleting an image from the Media library that is still used on a published service or portfolio page, causing broken images and hurting Lighthouse SEO. | Medium | High | Enforce a `beforeDelete` hook on the `Media` collection that queries referencing tables (`Pages`, `Services`, `Projects`, `Cities`, `Posts`, `SiteSettings`) and blocks deletion if referenced. |
| **RSK-010** | **Unintended Schema Drift Between Staging & Production DBs:** Modifying Payload fields without running migrations in PostgreSQL. | Low | High | Disable `push: true` outside local dev (`NODE_ENV === 'development'`) and enforce `payload migrate` as an automated pre-deploy step in the CI/CD pipeline (`20-SECURITY-PERFORMANCE-AND-DEVOPS.md`). |
