## 3. Client Account Ownership & Onboarding Governance (`BR-011`)

A core trust pillar of Bayyora across Home, Services, About, FAQ, and Terms is: **`كل الحسابات باسمك وملكك` / `Every account is in your name and stays yours`**.
1. **Domains & Hosting / E-Commerce Stores (Salla, Zid, Shopify):** Registered directly under the shop owner's legal name, commercial registration (CR), and email/phone. Bayyora accesses via delegated staff/partner permissions.
2. **Google Business Profile (Google Maps):** The shop owner is Always designated as **Primary Owner**; Bayyora is added as **Manager**.
3. **Social Media Accounts (Snapchat, Instagram, TikTok, X):** Owned by the client's phone/email; Bayyora manages publishing and replies without holding the client's accounts hostage.
4. **Advertising Accounts (Snap Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, Google Ads):** Created in the client's name with the client's direct payment method attached for ad spend (`BR-012`), ensuring complete financial transparency between Bayyora's service fee and media spend.
5. **Source Files Handover:** Upon final approval and settlement of Brand/Print projects, print-ready and open source files are handed over to the client.

## 4. Regulatory & Industry Compliance Framework (`BR-014`)

### 4.1 Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)
- **Scope:** Applies to all personal data collected via `/free-audit/`, `/contact/`, WhatsApp inquiries, and tracking pixels (`GA4`, `Meta Pixel`, `Snapchat Pixel`).
- **Mandatory Controls (Documented in `/privacy/`):**
  1. Clear disclosure of **what data is collected** (Name, Shop Name, City, WhatsApp/Phone, Social links, IP/cookie identifiers) and **why** (to deliver the requested Free Mini Audit, schedule shop visits, send quotes, and measure ad performance).
  2. Strict **no-sale guarantee:** Bayyora never sells or rents client or lead data to third parties.
  3. **Data Subject Rights under Saudi PDPL:** Right to be informed, right to access personal data, right to request correction/updating, right to request destruction/deletion of data, and right to withdraw marketing consent at any time via WhatsApp or email.

### 4.2 SFDA Compliance for Pharmacy & Medical Clients
- Because pharmacies are a primary target segment in Al Ahsa (`Doc A` §1), `Doc A` §2 mandates:
  - *"pharmacy and medicine content follows SFDA rules (our partner confirms before we publish)"*
- **Operational Gate (`OPS-COMP-01`):** No social media post, video, ad, or website claim for a pharmacy, clinic, health supplement, or medical product may be published without **explicit pre-publication verification by Bayyora's local partner** against Saudi Food & Drug Authority (SFDA / هيئة الغذاء والدواء) advertising regulations (and Ministry of Health licensing rules where applicable).

### 4.3 Mawthooq (موثوق) Licence Verification for Influencer Work
- Per `Doc A` §2: *"influencer work needs a Mawthooq licence check"*.
- **Operational Gate (`OPS-COMP-02`):** Before booking or recommending any local influencer or content creator in Al Ahsa, Dammam, or Khobar on behalf of a client, Bayyora must verify and log the influencer's active **Mawthooq (موثوق)** licence issued by the General Authority of Media Regulation (GAMR / الهيئة العامة لتنظيم الإعلام).

### 4.4 Municipality Signage Awareness (`Print & Shop Design`)
- For shopfront and signboard designs (`/services/print-shop-design/`), Bayyora delivers print-ready design files aligned with local Saudi municipal (`بلدي / Balady`) commercial signage guidelines (such as prominent Arabic lettering and compliant dimensions) and coordinates file handover to the client's chosen local printer.

## 6. CMS-Enforced Operational & Compliance Gates (Payload CMS)

To prevent human error, the Payload CMS Admin Dashboard enforces the following compliance rules in software:
1. **Legal Page Publishing Restriction (`RBAC`):** Only the `Owner / Super Admin` role (and authorized `Administrator`) can publish changes to `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/refunds`; `Content Editor` and `SEO Manager` roles have read/draft access only.
2. **Client Permission Gate (`Projects.clientPermissionVerified`):** Real client projects (such as **Dawair / دوائر**) cannot be switched from `isSampleConcept: true` to published real case studies unless `clientPermissionVerified` is checked and the permission note/date is recorded.
3. **SFDA Pharmacy Content Gate (`sfdaVerified`):** Any `Projects` or `Posts` item tagged under `Business Type: Pharmacy (صيدلية)` requires the `sfdaComplianceVerified` checkbox to be signed off before publication.
