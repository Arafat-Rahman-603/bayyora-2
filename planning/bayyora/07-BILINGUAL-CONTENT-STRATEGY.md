## 1. Voice, Tone & Linguistic Governance

### 1.1 Voice & Tone Rules (`[PROPOSED — PENDING APPROVAL OD-004]`)
- **Persona:** Like a helpful neighbour who knows marketing — never a cold, corporate agency.
- **Sentence Structure:** Short sentences. Simple, warm, Saudi-friendly Arabic (`لهجة بيضاء / سعودية قريبة وبسيطة`), avoiding stiff, overly formal classical phrasing or awkward literal translations. Plain, direct English on `/en/`.
- **Direct Address:** Speak directly to the shop owner using **`محلّك`** / **"your shop"**.
- **Concrete Over Hype:** *"More people find your shop on Google Maps"* (`خلّ الناس تلقاك في خرائط قوقل`) beats *"360° omnichannel digital transformation"*.
- **Conversation First:** Every CTA invites a low-pressure chat (a free mini audit, a WhatsApp message, or a shop visit) — never pushing price lists.
- **Strictly Prohibited Language:**
  - Marketing jargon (*funnels, KPIs, ROI, omni-channel, synergies*).
  - Fake urgency (*"Only 2 spots left today!", countdown timers*).
  - Guaranteed sales or ranking promises (*"Guaranteed #1 on Google in 7 days", "Double your sales"*).
  - Invented numbers, unverified statistics, fake reviews, or unapproved client logos.

### 1.2 Orthographic & Formatting Rules (`[CONFIRMED]`)
1. **The Shadda Rule:** Always write **بيّورا** with the shadda over the *yā'* (`يّ`). Also preserve natural Saudi vocalization shaddas used in the source copy (`محلّك`, `نخلّي`, `توقّف`, `المبرّز`).
2. **Arabic-First Presentation:** When Arabic and English appear together in a shared visual element (such as the logo lockup or slogan card), the Arabic line is always on top or first, with English smaller underneath.
3. **100% Bilingual Parity:** `/en/` is a complete, idiomatic English equivalent of every Arabic page — never a truncated summary page.
4. **Native Saudi Review Gate:** All Arabic copy in `Doc A` is marked as a **draft** and must pass review by a native Saudi Arabic speaker (and local partner sign-off) before launch.

## 3. Shared vs. Page-Specific Content Reuse Rules

To prevent accidental duplicate-content penalties while maintaining consistency across the site:
1. **9 Service Cards Block:** The concise 9 service cards (Title + 1-line benefit statement from `Doc A` §3.1) may be reused on **Home (`/`)** and **Services Hub (`/services/`)**. On the **5 City Pages (`/city/*`)**, the 9 services list must be contextualized to that city's commercial character (e.g., cafés and retail in Khobar; Qaisariya souq and growing neighbourhoods in Al Ahsa; restaurants and shops in Hofuf).
2. **Homepage Quick Questions vs. `/faq/`:** Homepage pulls 4–5 core questions from `/faq/`. Only `/faq/` (and the specific 3–4 service FAQs on each service page) should carry `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema where appropriate.
3. **Sample Concept Labelling Rule (`BR-009`):** Every conceptual Before/After card across Home, all 9 Service pages, Our Work, and City pages must render a persistent visual pill badge:
   - Arabic: **`نموذج توضيحي`**
   - English: **`Sample concept`**
   This badge may only be removed for a specific item when real client work is uploaded with documented written client permission.

## 5. Payload CMS Bilingual Localization & Translation Governance (`CMS-009`)

1. **Field-Level Localization Configuration:**
   - Payload CMS is configured with `localization: { locales: [{ label: 'العربية (Arabic)', code: 'ar', rtl: true }, { label: 'English', code: 'en', rtl: false }], defaultLocale: 'ar', fallback: false }`.
   - **Why `fallback: false` in Production Queries:** Setting `fallback: false` prevents untranslated Arabic strings from silently leaking onto `/en/` English pages (or vice versa) and prevents search engines from indexing mixed-language duplicate pages.
2. **Independent Per-Locale Translation Status & Native Saudi Review Gate:**
   - Every localized collection (`Pages`, `Services`, `Packages`, `Projects`, `Cities`, `Posts`, `Faqs`) includes a localized `translationStatus` field (`draft` | `pending_saudi_review` | `approved` | `published`) and a computed `translationCompleteness` indicator in the Admin Dashboard list view (`AR: 100% ✅ | EN: 100% ✅`).
   - **Pre-Publish Validation Hook (`beforeChange`):** A document cannot be marked `published` for a locale unless all required localized content fields, image `alt` texts, and SEO metadata (`metaTitle`, `metaDescription`) for that locale are populated and pass validation.
   - **Future Language Extensibility:** Adding a third language in the future requires only adding the locale descriptor to `src/i18n/config.ts` and `payload.config.ts` — zero database schema redesign or component refactoring.
